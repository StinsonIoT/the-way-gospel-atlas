# Atlas Implementation Roadmap (proposal, not authorization)

## Stage 0 — Safeguard and audit
Review current repository, existing scene IDs, map layers, route behavior, reading drawer, iPhone experience, build/test scripts and deployed site. Record current branch/commit and ensure rollback. No code changes until approved.

## Stage 1 — Evidence and inventory
Source register with scholarly/archaeological references, Bible passages, licensing; inventory places/events/movements; uncertainty and chronology matrix. Verify BSB excerpts. Do not fill gaps with fabricated data.

## Stage 2 — Two prototypes
Design regional overview and early first-century Jerusalem close-up using the same visual system. Provide map mockups, source-backed geometry plan, route rationale, iPhone/desktop previews and an explicit uncertainty legend.

## Stage 3 — Remaining geography
Galilee teaching/ministry; Holy Week sequence; resurrection/ascension scenes. Treat debated sites as debated and avoid unrecorded itinerary lines.

## Stage 4 — Exploration and storyboard
Short interactive panels for Bethlehem, Nazareth, Capernaum, Jerusalem and Gethsemane; source links; return-to-scene navigation; scroll storyboard with framing, text placement, animation and fallbacks.

## Stage 5 — MapLibre-ready handoff
Stable IDs, qualified place table, route table, regions/uncertainty geometries and source properties, attribution/licensing register, separate historical overlays and concept art. Do not mistake illustrations for georeferenced data.

## Stage 6 — Optional integration after approval
Small scoped PRs, no wholesale replacement. Validate typecheck/build, route/scene consistency, iPhone portrait/landscape, keyboard and screen-reader access, reduced motion, performance, loading fallback, and regression behavior. Record results and rollback steps.

## Definition of done
Deliver all 15 categories in docs/ATLAS-MASTER-PLAN.md; check citations, historical confidence, responsive readability and license compliance. Unverified work must be clearly marked as pending, never quietly treated as complete.

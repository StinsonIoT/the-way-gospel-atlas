# The Way — A Gospel Atlas: Project Vision

Website: https://stinsoniot.github.io/the-way-gospel-atlas/
Repository: https://github.com/StinsonIoT/the-way-gospel-atlas

## Purpose
Tell the story of Jesus' birth, life, ministry, teaching, death, resurrection and ascension in a beautiful, source-backed, immersive atlas. Visitors should understand **what happened and where it happened**. Scrolling reveals narrative and geography; Explore mode allows deeper study of places, passages, evidence and uncertainty.

## Audiences
- First-time Gospel readers: welcoming, intuitive and easy to follow.
- Bible students: evidence, cross-references, archaeological context, scholarly disagreement and source links.
- iPhone visitors: clear text, touch-friendly controls, uncluttered viewport, stable navigation.

## Experience principles
1. Scripture and narrative lead; cartography supports comprehension.
2. Emotional impact comes from geography, light, depth and pacing—not fantasy ornament or exaggerated certainty.
3. Distinguish evidence from illustration at every scale.
4. Quiet background maps for reading; detailed interactive maps for exploration.
5. Preserve story position when opening/closing Explore or Scripture.
6. Maintain the existing website and completed work. Any integration requires separate approval.

## Deliverables
Coordinated regional, Galilee, first-century Jerusalem, Holy Week, resurrection and ascension maps; shared legend; desktop/iPhone previews; scroll storyboard; example panels for Bethlehem, Nazareth, Capernaum, Jerusalem and Gethsemane; source register; place and route tables; qualified MapLibre-ready GeoJSON; unresolved historical questions.

## Project boundary
This documentation is a research/design brief and handoff to Claude Code/Codex. It is not an instruction to immediately modify the website.

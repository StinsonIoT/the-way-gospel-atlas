# Complete Atlas Master Plan

This is the full project brief, not a reduced task list. Read alongside PROJECT-VISION.md, HISTORICAL-ACCURACY.md, MAP-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md and IMPLEMENTATION-ROADMAP.md.

## 1. Accuracy
Four Gospels and Acts 1; verified BSB excerpts; reliable atlases, archaeological research and scholarly disagreement. Every event separates text, evidence, tradition, proposals and unknowns. No fabricated roads, coordinates, dates, sequences or architectural certainty. Preserve meaningful Gospel chronology differences.

## 2. Shared identity
Warm ivory/limestone; sage/olive; muted blue-green water; restrained antique gold; charcoal-green text; subtle relief; elegant serif headings. Quiet reading maps and detailed Explore maps with shared markers, routes and uncertainty notation.

## 3. Regional overview
Geographical scope for birth, flight to Egypt, ministry, northern regions and Judea. Consider Bethlehem, Nazareth, Jerusalem, Judea, Samaria, Galilee, Jordan, Sea of Galilee, Dead Sea, Egypt region, Cana, Capernaum, Bethsaida, Chorazin, Nain, Sychar/Jacob's well, Jericho, Bethany near Jerusalem, Bethphage, Mount of Olives, Tyre, Sidon, Decapolis and Caesarea Philippi district. Verify all identifications; distinguish Bethany references. Avoid all-labels-at-once. Egypt is a regional destination without an invented route.

## 4. Galilee
Lake, hills, valleys, communities, corridors. Narratives: kingdom announcement, disciples, teaching/parables, healing, encounters, lake crossings, preparation. Explicit care with Bethsaida, feeding miracles, Sermon on Mount/Plain, transfiguration and Gerasene/Gadarene shore. Emphasize why Capernaum matters.

## 5. First-century Jerusalem
Temple precinct, contemporary walls/gates and proposed alignments, Kidron, Mount of Olives, Gethsemane, Bethesda, Siloam, southern Temple approaches, high-priestly areas, Pilate trial proposals, Golgotha/tomb and Last Supper traditions. Separate what Scripture states from locatable evidence and tradition. Avoid anachronistic streets, later walls and the modern Via Dolorosa as a certain path. Any 3D model is explicitly reconstruction.

## 6. Holy Week
Bethany/Bethphage approach, entry, Jerusalem teaching, Temple, supper, Mount of Olives/Gethsemane, arrest, hearings/trials, crucifixion, burial. Narrative transitions and route uncertainty explicit. Conventional/debated day assignments labeled. Progressive camera from region to city; solemn restrained visuals.

## 7. Resurrection and ascension
Tomb, Jerusalem, Emmaus road (disputed destination), Galilee, Sea of Tiberias, unnamed Galilee mountain, vicinity of Bethany/Mount of Olives. Distinguish appearances, recorded disciple journeys and editorial connections. Preserve Luke 24 / Acts 1 wording. No invented itinerary for Jesus.

## 8. Route and scroll grammar
Separate scripturally recorded movements with unknown path, sourced plausible routes, editorial connections, broad regions and disputed destinations. Show current segment by default, optional overview. Gentle animation; responsive scroll, reduced motion and static fallback.

## 9. Explore panels
Biblical name, modern identification, confidence, first-century context, Jesus' actions/teaching, Gospel passages, verified BSB excerpts, archaeology, traditions/disputes, sources and return to story. Short overview first, deep research on demand. Organize multiple city events.

## 10. iPhone first
Background map plus readable foreground, local softening, unobscured selected places, compact controls, safe areas, orientation changes, no horizontal overflow or hover dependency. Journey: Begin → scroll/read → Scripture → Explore → return. Keyboard access, contrast and loading fallback.

## 11. MapLibre readiness
Separate layers: land/water, terrain, regions, historical city features, places, labels, routes, scene highlights, uncertainty, selection. True geographic data and longitude-latitude GeoJSON. Document Jerusalem overlay alignment. Record provider/license/attribution/key requirements for all external data.

## 12. Image generation limits
Permitted for moodboards, background texture and labeled illustrative reconstructions. Not for coordinates, historical boundaries, roads, buildings, archaeology, labels or chronology. Never present concept art as validated maps.

Stable IDs must integrate with existing story scenes. Prefer areas or candidates over misleading point precision.

## 14. Workflow
Inspect existing website/repository and report what was actually inspected. Establish evidence framework → inventory → style → regional and Jerusalem prototypes → remaining maps → biblical/geographic review → iPhone and interaction review → deliver assets and implementation specs. No site/repo modification unless separately authorized. Do not silently reduce scope; work in documented stages.
